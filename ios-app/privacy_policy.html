Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Centsnap ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

Centsnap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data you enter (transactions, budgets, settings) is stored locally on your device using local storage. We do not have access to this data.

No Account Required

Centsnap does not require an account or registration. No email, name, or other personal information is collected.

Third-Party Services

Centsnap does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Export & Deletion

You can export all your data as CSV from the app's Settings tab. To delete all data, use the "Reset All Data" option in the app or delete the app from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
